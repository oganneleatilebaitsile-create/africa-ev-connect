Africa EV Connect ⚡🌍

Connecting Africa’s Electric Mobility Future

Africa EV Connect is an independent digital platform designed to connect electric vehicle businesses, charging providers, suppliers, fleet operators and customers across Botswana and Southern Africa.

Our mission is to make electric mobility information easier to access and help businesses discover opportunities, services and potential partnerships.

🚀 Features

- Business Directory: Discover EV businesses and service providers.
- Opportunities: Explore electric mobility developments and potential business opportunities.
- Supplier Connections: Submit enquiries about EV products, charging solutions and related services.
- Business Enquiries: Connect with potential suppliers and service providers.
- Verification Labels: Distinguish verified listings from unverified information and research leads.
- Mobile-Friendly Design: Access the platform using a smartphone, tablet or computer.

🌍 Target Market

- Botswana
- South Africa
- Namibia
- Zambia
- Other African markets as the platform grows

🔒 Transparency and Disclaimer

Africa EV Connect is an independent project. It is not a government agency and does not claim endorsement by any government, vehicle manufacturer or organisation.

A listing or opportunity notice does not guarantee accuracy, safety, funding, contracts or commercial success. Users should verify information directly with the relevant provider or official source.
